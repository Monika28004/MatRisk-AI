# MatRisk AI

Dual-Domain AI System Integrating Material Science and Financial Risk Analytics

## Problem Statement

Material degradation and structural failures can cause significant financial losses in commodity markets, infrastructure projects, insurance portfolios, and ESG investments.

Traditional systems analyze material risks and financial risks separately.

MatRisk AI bridges these domains by translating physical material risks into measurable financial impacts.

## Objectives

1. Design a dual-domain architecture.
2. Encode crystal structure properties.
3. Predict material performance.
4. Model degradation and failure risk.
5. Quantify commodity and infrastructure risk.
6. Estimate insurance catastrophe exposure.
7. Analyze ESG portfolio impact.
8. Simulate risk scenarios through MatRisk Lab.
9. Validate system performance.

## Methodology

Material Data
      ↓
Crystal Encoding
      ↓
Property Prediction
      ↓
Failure Modeling
      ↓
Financial Risk Analysis
      ↓
Insurance & ESG Analytics
      ↓
Simulation
      ↓
Validation
