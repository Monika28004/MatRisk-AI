# MatRisk AI System Overview

MatRisk AI is a dual-domain artificial intelligence platform that bridges material science and financial risk analytics.

The system predicts how material degradation, structural failure, and physical performance affect financial outcomes across commodity markets, infrastructure projects, insurance portfolios, and ESG investments.

## Core Domains

### Material Science Domain
- Crystal Structure Encoding
- Property Prediction
- Failure Modeling
- Degradation Quantification

### Financial Risk Domain
- Commodity Trading Risk
- Infrastructure Finance Risk
- Insurance Catastrophe Pricing
- ESG Portfolio Analytics

## Final Objective

Translate physical material risks into measurable financial risks using AI-driven modeling and simulation.
